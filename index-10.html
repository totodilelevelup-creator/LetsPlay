HOW TO PLAY

The Duel

Both players begin with 4000 LP and draw five cards. You draw one card at the start of your turn. Summon one monster per turn, then attack or use effects.

Levels 1–4 need no tribute; Levels 5–6 need one; Levels 7–8 need two; Levels 9–10 need three. Tribute monsters are sent to the Graveyard.

Attack Position monsters use ATK in battle. Defence Position monsters use DEF. If an attacker loses to an enemy monster, the difference is dealt as damage to its controller. Direct attacks are possible if the opposing monster field is empty.

Play a spell by selecting it and pressing Activate. Set a trap to a Spell/Trap Zone. A set trap triggers automatically when its condition is met. Win by reducing the opponent to 0 LP or making them unable to draw.

Tap a card in your hand to select it. Tap a monster in your field to select it for attacks, position changes or tribute. Then choose the matching action.
